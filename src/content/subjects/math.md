## Course model

Math content is organized into grade pathways, concept groups, lessons, fluency routines, games, and diagnostics. Each pathway stores its expected concept count in frontmatter so the generated route can be checked against the Markdown concept library.

## Teaching formats

- Mini-lessons introduce vocabulary and models.
- Guided practice gives immediate feedback and hints.
- Fluency rounds build automaticity.
- Games rehearse concepts in a lower-pressure format.
- Diagnostics identify the next useful concept.
