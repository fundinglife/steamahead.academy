## Inspiration hub

The Inspiration area is a resource hub for getting started, building routines, finding family examples, celebrating learners, and downloading practical materials.

## Getting started

- **Quick-start guide**: a short introduction to the platform’s major features.
- **Family guide**: simple routines for using practice, recommendations, diagnostics, and progress views at home.

## Explore learning

- **Personalized recommendations** help a learner begin with skills matched to progress.
- **Subject browsing** lets learners explore math, language arts, science, social studies, and Spanish skills directly.
- **Diagnostic levels** reveal current knowledge and generate action plans for growth.

## Resources

- Family learning guidance.
- SmartScore explanation and goal-setting support.
- Video tips and product walkthroughs.
- User guides for accounts, assignments, reports, and implementation.

## Find inspiration

- **Family stories** show how real routines can support confidence and consistency.
- **IXL Rockstars** recognize learners who complete large practice milestones.
- **Printable resources** include certificates, scavenger hunts, and other family learning tools.
- **Blog resources** provide ongoing ideas for classroom and home use.
