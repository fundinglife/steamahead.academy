## Assessment model

This page represents the same assessment-suite role as the source site: find what learners know, identify gaps, and connect results to exact next-step practice.

## Assessment suite

- **Universal screener**: a short adaptive screen for identifying students who may need intervention in math or reading.
- **Benchmark diagnostic**: scheduled assessment windows for capturing starting levels, checking growth, and comparing progress over time.
- **Real-Time Diagnostic**: a continuously updating view of math and ELA proficiency that can stay current through brief weekly diagnostic work.
- **Common assessments**: unit or course checks that help teachers evaluate recent instruction.
- **Personalized action plans**: skill recommendations that translate assessment results into immediate practice.

## From assessment to instruction

1. Screen for intervention needs.
2. Run a benchmark to establish current levels.
3. Assign action-plan skills that address knowledge gaps.
4. Use ongoing diagnostic work to keep recommendations current.
5. Review analytics to group students and plan reteaching.

## What the page must connect to

Assessment is not a standalone test page. It links forward to diagnostic experiences, recommendations, skill practice, and analytics so each result has a next instructional step.
