## Analytics model

Analytics turns practice and assessment activity into instructional decisions. The source page is organized around the questions teachers ask during planning, grouping, intervention, and progress reporting.

## Whole-class instruction

**Question:** What should I teach next?

Reports show progress toward proficiency across standards, skills, classes, and individual students. The equivalent page should help a teacher see what the class understands and where reteaching will have the highest impact.

## Small group differentiation

**Question:** Who needs reteaching, and on what?

Analytics groups students by need and surfaces the question types they are missing. This supports targeted small-group instruction instead of requiring a teacher to manually sort every result.

## One-to-one intervention

**Question:** What misconception is blocking this student?

Student-level views preserve answered-question history, missed patterns, and evidence for review conferences. The purpose is to move from a low score to a specific misconception and next action.

## Progress sharing

**Question:** How do I show growth to families and administrators?

Progress views summarize achievements, growth, and current needs so teachers can prepare conferences, IEP meetings, administrator updates, and family conversations.

## More analytics workflows

- Take post-assessment action by connecting diagnostic scores to exact recommended skills.
- Check assignment completion from a single progress grid.
- Monitor standards proficiency over time.
- Identify students who need review, enrichment, or a new challenge.
