## Curriculum pathway

Takeoff is the core-curriculum pathway: a K-5 math program that combines whole-class instruction, differentiated support, online practice, assessments, and analytics in one course sequence.

## Daily lesson plans

Each unit is built around day-by-day lessons for whole-class teaching. A lesson includes an objective, concept review, warm-up, instructional activities, and teacher guidance for supporting students who need extra help.

## Differentiated support and enrichment

The pathway includes resources for students who need reteaching and students ready for challenge work. The goal is to keep the class on grade-level content while giving each learner an appropriate level of support.

## Adaptive online skills

Online skills reinforce each lesson objective with immediate feedback and adaptive question difficulty. Students can use connected recommendations to fill gaps or extend learning.

## Assessments and next steps

Mid-unit checks and end-of-unit tests identify which concepts students understood and which need review. After each check, the pathway should surface small groups and recommended content for personalization days.

## Integrated curriculum tools

- Lesson plans and teacher materials.
- Print and online student activities.
- Video tutorials for key math concepts.
- Educational games and classroom engagement tools.
- Analytics that connect activity, performance, and next steps.
