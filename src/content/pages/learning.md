## Learning hub

This hub mirrors the public learning menu structure: subject skills first, then supporting learning formats and personalized pathways.

## Subject pathways

- **Math**: grade and course pathways from Pre-K through Calculus, plus lessons, videos, games, and Fluency Zone practice.
- **Language arts**: skills by grade, plus reading and language arts videos and games.
- **Science**: elementary grade bands plus middle school, biology, chemistry, and physics.
- **Social studies**: grade bands plus U.S. history and civics pathways.
- **Spanish**: skill groups for vocabulary, grammar, and contextual Spanish practice.

## Learning formats

- **Skills**: adaptive practice pages organized by subject, grade, course, and concept.
- **Lessons**: short instructional pages that introduce or review math concepts before practice.
- **Videos**: guided explanations connected to math and language arts topics.
- **Games**: early-learning practice formats for math and language arts concepts.
- **Fluency Zone**: fast math fact practice for building automaticity.

## Personalized pathways

- **Recommendations** connect learners to skills that match recent work and readiness.
- **Skill plans** organize exact skills for state standards, textbooks, and test preparation.
- **Awards** recognize completed work and encourage continued practice.
