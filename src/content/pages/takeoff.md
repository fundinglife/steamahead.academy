## Core curriculum pathway

Takeoff is the core-curriculum pathway for elementary math. It combines daily whole-class instruction, differentiated resources, adaptive online skills, assessments, and reporting into one connected course sequence.

## Daily lesson plans

Each lesson includes an objective, concept background, warm-up, instructional activities, and teacher moves for guiding the class through grade-level math.

## Differentiated support

Support and enrichment resources are built into the lesson sequence so teachers can reteach, extend, and respond without leaving the course plan.

## Adaptive online skills

Students reinforce lesson concepts through online practice with feedback, adaptive difficulty, and connected recommendations for filling knowledge gaps.

## Assessments and next steps

Mid-unit check-ins and end-of-unit tests identify what students understand and what needs attention next. The page links assessment results to small groups, recommended materials, and personalization days.

## Integrated tools

- Print and online learning activities.
- Video tutorials for key math concepts.
- Educational games and collaborative classroom activities.
- Analytics that help teachers act on student performance.
